Homeboard terms of use

Last updated 3 November 2026

Who it's for

Homeboard is for households in the United Kingdom. It is not offered, supported or intended for use anywhere else. You need a Google account, an authenticator app and an invite from someone already in your household.

Your household

  • Whoever sets up a household is its owner. The owner and admins can invite people, change cost-sharing groups, and pause or remove members.
  • Anything you share is visible to everyone in your household. Private lists, private events and reminders are visible only to you.
  • Only invite people you live with or share costs with, and keep your sign-in and authenticator to yourself.

Bin days, reminders and budgets

Bin collection days come from your council or from what you enter, and can change without notice, especially around bank holidays. Check your council if it matters. Reminders depend on your phone and its connection, so don't rely on them for anything critical. The budget splits costs by the headcounts your household sets. It is a record to help you settle up, not financial advice.

The service

Homeboard is provided as it is, while it's being developed. Features may change, and it may sometimes be unavailable. Company logos shown for bills belong to their owners. Homeboard isn't connected with or endorsed by those companies.
