Homeboard privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Homeboard is a private app for households in the United Kingdom to share a calendar, shopping lists, to-do lists, reminders and a budget. Only people invited by a member of the household can use it. It is not offered outside the UK. See also the terms of use.

What we store

  • Your name, email address and Google account ID from Google sign-in.
  • What you add to the app: events, lists, to-dos, reminders and budget entries.
  • A device token, if you turn on notifications on Android.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: access to your primary calendar's events, and a token that lets the app keep them in sync.
  • Your household's bin collection days. If you look them up, your postcode is used for that search only and isn't stored.

How it's used

Only to run the app for your household: showing your information to you, and showing what you choose to share to the other members. Events imported from Google Calendar are visible only to you unless you mark one as shared. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the household.

Google user data

Homeboard reads and writes events in your primary Google Calendar only to keep it in step with Homeboard. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time from the Calendar screen, which deletes the stored token and the imported events. You can also remove access in your Google account settings.

Where it's kept, and who else sees it

Data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and notifications are sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Access is protected by Google sign-in plus an authenticator code.

When you look up bin days, your postcode goes to postcodes.io, a free service built on Office for National Statistics data, to find your council. Where Homeboard can read your council's bin search for you, the postcode and the address you pick go to that council too. Neither is kept by Homeboard.

Your rights

UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) applies. You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data Homeboard holds about you. If you're unhappy with how it's handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Deleting your data

Ask your household's owner or an admin to remove you, which deletes your private lists, events and reminders. To have everything about you deleted, including your account, contact us using the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
